Our Origins • Meiji Era

Born from Kyoto’s River Mists & Ancient Cedar Groves

In the autumn of 1892, Master Toru Hoshino established a modest wooden cupping house along the banks of the Uji River in southern Kyoto. Witnessing the rapid industrialization of tea processing that shredded delicate tender buds, Hoshino swore an oath never to commoditize the leaf.

He insisted on unbroken, hand-harvested flushes, pan-firing them in small charcoal-heated iron woks to capture volatile floral aromatics. Four generations later, the Hoshino family preserves those exact thermodynamic standards, expanding our terroir stewardship to Taiwan’s mist-shrouded Alishan peaks and the high elevations of Sri Lanka.

The Verdant Quadrant

Our Four Inviolable Principles

Every harvest that bears the Verdant wax seal must strictly satisfy our four core covenants of botanical ethics and thermodynamic precision.

01 / BOTANY

100% Unbroken Whole Leaf

We strictly ban machine CTC crushed dust, fannings, or industrial sweeping remnants. Only whole, intact two-leaves-and-a-bud flushes enter our tins.

Zero Chemical Binders
02 / HUMANITY

Direct-Trade Artisan Equity

We circumvent commercial auction floors. We negotiate directly with multigenerational farm families, paying on average 300% above Fairtrade certified floors.

Guaranteed Farm Direct
03 / SCIENCE

Nitrogen Cold-Chain Vault

Oxygen is the assassin of volatile catechins. Our harvests are vacuum sealed at source, flushed with 99.9% food-grade inert nitrogen, and warehoused at 4°C.

Cryogenic Freshness
04 / SENSORY

Sommelier Cupping Oath

Before any batch is cleared for consumer release, our master cuppers blind-test liquor density, umami finish, dry-leaf aroma, and wet-leaf elasticity across 5 steepings.

5-Steep Cupping Protocol

Living Ecology

Regenerative Soil, Pure Glacier Runoff & High Altitude Biodiversity

We reject the monoculture wasteland of commercial mass plantations. Every estate in our partner network practices biodynamic permaculture. Deep-rooted indigenous cedar and camphor trees shade the tea bushes, preventing topsoil erosion and providing habitats for beneficial predator insects.

Glacial Spring Hydration

Tea leaves absorb the chemical memory of their water source. Our partner gardens are exclusively fed by mineral-pure sub-alpine springs.

Zero Synthetic Inputs

Soil nutrients are replenished using fermented mountain compost, fermented rapeseed oil cake, and mountain leaf mulch.

1892 • The Founding Cupping House

Master Toru Hoshino Pioneers Whole-Leaf Pan-Firing Along the Uji River

Refusing to participate in high-speed mechanical shredding introduced during Japan's late Meiji modernization, Toru Hoshino builds our first charcoal-fired atelier in Uji, proving that intact, undamaged leaf cells produce substantially sweeter liquor with minimal astringency.

Artifact: The original hand-written 1892 Pan-Firing Temperature Manuscript is preserved in our Kyoto vault.
Technical Rigor

Our Nitrogen Preservation & Sensory Testing Laboratory

From the moment fresh whole leaves complete their pan-firing or roasting curve, biological degradation begins if exposed to oxygen, humidity, or temperatures above 18°C.

To safeguard the delicate botanical aromatics, our Kyoto and flagship distribution hubs feature hermetically sealed, cold nitrogen vaults. Here, relative humidity is locked at precisely 42%, and inert nitrogen gas displaces air in every single bag and tin before sealing.
