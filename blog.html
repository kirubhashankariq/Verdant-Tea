The 3,000-Year Ancient Arbor: Decoding the Terroir of Mount Da Xue Shan

High in the mist canopy of Lincang, semi-wild Camellia arbors have witnessed three millennia of climatic cycles. We climbed to 2,400 meters to document how deep root tap systems absorb volcanic basalt minerals, yielding a liquor that lingers for twenty minutes in the palate.

Arbor Age & Origin 800–3,200 Yrs • Lincang
Alpine Terroir 2,400m Basalt Strata
Aroma Notes Wild Honey • Camphor

Astronomical Agronomy

The 24 Solar Nodes Plucking Calendar

In ancestral tea farming, plucking dates are governed by solar coordinates, not commercial delivery schedules. A difference of seventy-two hours around Qingming permanently shifts the amino-acid profile.

TERM 05 / 24 Peak Active

Qingming (Pure Brightness)

April 04 – April 19

The most revered plucking window. Tender buds awake from winter dormancy with concentrated L-theanine and zero bitterness.

Harvested: Uji Gyokuro • Darjeeling First Flush
TERM 06 / 24 Upcoming

Guyu (Grain Rain)

April 20 – May 05

Warm showers stimulate leaf broadness and natural essential oils. Perfect for strip-rolled high mountain oolongs.

Harvested: Alishan Oolong • Phoenix Dan Cong
TERM 15 / 24 Vault Curing

Bailu (White Dew)

September 07 – September 22

Crisp dry mountain winds gently reduce moisture in the leaves prior to secondary charcoal curing over longan embers.

Harvested: Wuyi Cliff Da Hong Pao • Rou Gui
TERM 21 / 24 Cellar Vault

Daxue (Great Snow)

December 07 – December 21

Subterranean resting period. Post-fermented ripe Pu-erh cakes are turned in climate-controlled micro-cellars.

Harvested: Menghai Vintage Pu-erh Pressing
