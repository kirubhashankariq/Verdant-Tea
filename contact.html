Curator Insights

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidance on storage, aging potential, temperature shipping, and terroir purity.

Store loose leaf teas in an airtight, opaque container away from light, heat, moisture, and strong kitchen aromas. Green teas and Matcha benefit from refrigeration in a sealed container, while Aged Pu-erhs and Oolongs should be kept at stable room temperature (18°C–22°C) with moderate humidity.
Delicate green teas (Sencha, Gyokuro) and Matcha are best consumed within 6 to 12 months of harvest to enjoy their fresh grassy chlorophyll. Conversely, Wuyi Rock Oolongs and compressed Pu-erhs have no expiration date; they gain depth, smoothness, and value over 20 to 30 years of careful aging.
During transit above 25°C, all delicate spring green teas and ceremonial matcha tins are dispatched in insulated thermal sleeves with eco-friendly chilling packs to prevent heat oxidation in parcel depots.
