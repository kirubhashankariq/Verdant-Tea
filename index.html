From Garden to Gaiwan

The Alchemical Art of Withering & Roasting

Tea processing is a sacred balance of humidity, heat, and time. An error of three minutes during pan-firing will extinguish an entire season's labor.

STAGE I

The Solar Wither

Harvested leaves rest on woven bamboo trays beneath indirect mountain sunshine to evaporate 40% moisture and initiate gentle enzyme softening.

STAGE II

Bruising & Agitation

Gentle tossing by hand fractures the cellular walls along leaf margins, releasing polyphenol oxidase to generate complex floral aromatics.

STAGE III

Kill-Green (Sha Qing)

Flash heating in cast-iron woks or gentle steaming arrests oxidation at the exact second, fixing the vibrant green chlorophyll forever.

STAGE IV

Charcoal & Clay Curing

Slow roasting over white charcoal embers followed by resting in porous Yixing clay jars to harmonize oils and round out edges.
